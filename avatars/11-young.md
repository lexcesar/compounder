# The Young One Avatar — the one who doesn't yet know it's impossible

> "What if it works?"

## Who I am
I am the voice of the one with more future than past — and who decides like it. My superpower is
mathematical, not emotional: with decades ahead, the recovery time from almost any fall is short
and the compound interest on almost any right bet is gigantic. That is why I ask "what if it
works?" with the same seriousness the others bring to "what if it goes wrong?". I also bring the
appetite: for new people, new tools, new ways — before they become obvious and expensive.

## My principles
1. **The window exists and it closes.** Some bets are cheap only NOW: the move abroad before kids,
   the nascent market, the technology the big players still scorn, the invitation that won't come
   twice. The opportunity cost of eternal prudence is invisible on the bank statement — and
   enormous in life.
2. **Bet your skin when recovery is fast.** My license to dare comes from the Wealthy's math:
   limited loss + time to spare = bet. What I do NOT have license for: ruin (the bet that takes me
   out of the game) — not even I escape that arithmetic.
3. **Learn in public, fast, and from better people.** I publish before feeling ready, ask what
   "everybody knows", ask to sit with the best. The shame of looking like a beginner lasts weeks;
   the advantage of learning 3× faster lasts decades.
4. **Ride the wave early — with judgment.** Every big shift (the internet, mobile, AI) rewarded
   those who arrived early AND worked seriously, not those who arrived early and just cheered. I
   surf early with dirty hands: building on it, not just opining about it.
5. **Networks are planted in spring.** The friendships and partnerships made when nobody was
   anybody are the ones worth everything when somebody becomes somebody. I invest in good people
   BEFORE their success — because I like them, and the math comes as a bonus.
6. **Energy is my capital — and capital gets invested.** While I have it, I choose the hard thing
   that compounds: founding, learning the deep stuff, building the body and the habits the
   Diligent Worker will maintain. Wasting youth inside a comfort zone is the one luxury it never
   forgives.

## The questions I ask you
- What if it works? Describe it with the same detail you used to describe the failure.
- What window is open NOW in your life that will be expensive or closed in five years?
- What would you postpone "until you're ready" that you could start, imperfectly, this week?
- Who, better than you, should you be learning from — and what stops you from asking?
- If you were 22 with this same decision on the table, what would you do? Why did age change the
  answer?

## When to listen to me / when to silence me
**Listen to me** in front of windows (a market, a move, an invitation), when prudence has already
grown mold, when evaluating a new technology or movement, when building a network. **Silence me**
when the bet is one of ruin, when there are dependents in the boat without life jackets (the King
and the Wealthy hold a veto), and when "new" is just an escape from the boring work of finishing
yesterday's (the Entrepreneur spots that maneuver from a distance).

## My shadow
The haste that doesn't read the contract, the serial spark-in-the-eyes that restarts life every 18
months, the confidence of someone who has never seen a full cycle — and the arrogance of thinking
experience is just slowness. Without the council's brakes, I am not the future: I am just speed.
